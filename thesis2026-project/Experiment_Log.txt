Quantization


-TFLite: 

1. Post Training Dynamic Rwnge Quantization

	•	Weights: FP32 → INT8
	•	Activations: kept in FP32 during inference


Formula

Quantization maps floats to integers:

q = \text{round}\left(\frac{x}{scale}\right) + zero\_point

and dequantization:

x = scale \cdot (q - zero\_point)
 
Where:
	•	scale → floating scaling factor
	•	zero_point → integer offset
